## Decisions worth a look in the browser

1. **Field borders are visibly stronger.** 3:1 needs about 55% opacity; every `.input` and `.select` changes.
2. **Position, OVR and stat pills now have dark text** on red, green and blue instead of white.
3. **Chips and avatars with 8 to 10px text became 11px.** Tight spots (20px avatar initials in `TransferFeed`, `MatchCenter` rows) may need spacing.
4. Scrims other than `BaseModal` lost their blur; they are 50 to 85% black so they should read the same.
